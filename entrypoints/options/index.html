PageCraft · AI settings

Choose which AI writes your themes. Keys are stored only in this browser's local extension storage and are sent only to the provider you pick.

What gets sent

When you press Generate, PageCraft sends your instructions, your current CSS (if “Refine current” is on) and a short page digest (URL without query string, title, DOM outline, colors, fonts, CSS variables) to the selected provider. No form values or page text content are sent.
